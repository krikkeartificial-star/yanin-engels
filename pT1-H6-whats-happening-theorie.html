⭐ 4. Present continuous: am / is / are + -ing

De present continuous gebruik je voor iets dat nu gebeurt. Vorm = to be (am / is / are) + werkwoord + -ing.

PersoonVoorbeeld
II am vacuuming the floor.
he / she / itShe is cleaning her room.
you / we / theyThey are watching TV.

-ing spelling: meestal +ing (work → working) · eindigend op -e: valt weg (make → making) · korte klank: dubbel de medeklinker (stop → stopping, jog → jogging) · op -ie: verander die in -ie → -y (die → dying, lie → lying) · op één klinker + l: dubbel de l (travel → travelling).

⭐ 5. Present continuous — ontkennen en vragen

Ontkenning: to be in de ontkennende vorm + -ing. I am not talking / I'm not talking · He is not talking / He isn't talking.

Vraag: zet am/is/are vooraan + onderwerp + -ing: Are you talking? Antwoord kort: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. Met een vraagwoord: What are you doing?

⭐ 6. Present simple tegenover present continuous

De present simple vertelt wat je gewoonlijk doet (gewoontes en feiten). De present continuous vertelt wat er nu gebeurt.

Gewoonte (simple)Nu (continuous)
I clean my bedroom every Saturday.Right now, I am cleaning my bedroom.
Dad cooks dinner every evening.Listen! Dad is cooking dinner.
She usually does the dishes.She is doing the dishes at the moment.

Signaalwoorden simple: always, usually, often, normally, sometimes, never, every day. Signaalwoorden continuous: now, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!.

In de present simple krijgt he/she/it een -s: Mr Grant works in an office. · She does the dishes.
