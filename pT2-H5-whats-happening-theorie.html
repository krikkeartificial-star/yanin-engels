Unit 4 · What's happening? — huishoudtaken en wat er nú gebeurt

In deze unit leer je vertellen over huishoudtaken (chores), de kamers en spullen in huis, en bovenal over wat er op dit moment gebeurt met de present continuous. Je ziet ook het verschil met de present simple (gewoonten en feiten).

1. Huishoudtaken (household chores)

  • to clean the windows (ramen kuisen) · to do the dishes / do the washing-up (afwassen) · to feed the pets (huisdieren voeren)
  • to clear the table (tafel afruimen) · to lay the table (tafel dekken) · to do the washing / laundry (de was doen)
  • to fold clothes (kleren vouwen) · to iron (clothes) (strijken) · to cook (koken)
  • to dust (afstoffen) · to hoover / to vacuum (stofzuigen) · to sweep the floor (vegen) · to mop the floor (dweilen)
  • to make the bed (bed opmaken) · to tidy up (opruimen) · to put out the dustbins / take out the rubbish (vuilnis buitenzetten)
  • to load the dishwasher (afwasmachine vullen) · to unload the dishwasher (afwasmachine ledigen) · to wash the car (auto wassen)
  • to mow the lawn (gras maaien) · to hang clothes on the clothesline (kleren ophangen)

2. Kamers in het huis (rooms)

an attic (zolder), a basement / a cellar (kelder), a bathroom (badkamer), a bedroom (slaapkamer), a dining room (eetkamer), a garden (tuin), a hall / hallway (hal), a kitchen (keuken), a living room (woonkamer), a storeroom / storage room (bergruimte), a study (studie-/werkruimte), a terrace (terras), a utility room (bijkeuken/werkruimte).

3. Schoonmaakspullen (cleaning supplies)

a broom (bezem), a bucket (emmer), a dustpan (stoffer en blik), an extendable duster (verlengbare stofdoek), an iron (strijkijzer), a mop (dweil), a scrub brush (schrobber), sponges (sponzen), a vacuum cleaner (stofzuiger), rubber gloves (rubberhandschoenen), a cleaning towel (schoonmaakdoek), a window cleaner / squeegee (trekker), detergent (US) / washing-up liquid (UK) (afwasmiddel), a garbage bag and bin (US) / a bin bag and bin (UK) (vuilniszak en -emmer), a warning sign (waarschuwingsbord).

4. Present continuous: wat gebeurt nú (positive)

Vorm: am / is / are + werkwoord-ing.

OnderwerpBevestigend (long / short)
Iam reading — I’m reading
you / we / theyare reading — they’re reading
he / she / itis reading — she’s reading

Voorbeelden: Look, Mr. Grant is reading a book in the living room. · Mia is vacuuming the floor.

5. Present continuous: ontkennend en vragend

Negatief: am/are/is + not: I am not working. / He is not arguing = He isn’t arguing. / They are not moving = They aren’t moving.

Vraag: begin met am/are/is voor het onderwerp: Are you talking? · Is she watching TV? Kort antwoord: Yes, I am. / No, he isn’t.

6. Spelling van de -ing-vorm

  • Gewoon +ing: to work → working, to read, to cook.
  • Eindigt op -e → -e weg, dan +ing: make → making, drive → driving, ride (behalve be → being, see → seeing).
  • Kort werkwoord, één klinker + één medeklinker → verdubbel: put → putting, stop → stopping, swim → swimming, jog → jogging.
  • Eindigt op -ie → -y: tie → tying, lie → lying, die → dying.
  • Eindigt op één klinker + l → verdubbel de l: travel → travelling, control → controlling, quarrel.

7. Present simple vs present continuous

Present simple = gewoonte, routine, feit (met always, usually, normally, often, every day, rarely, never): Mr. Grant works at a local office. Every morning he drinks his coffee. · Present continuous = nu, op dit moment (met now, at the moment, right now, Look!, Listen!): Look, Mr. Grant is reading a book.

Vergelijk: Maeve usually does the dishes after dinner. (gewoonte) → Is Maeve doing the dishes now? No, she is mowing the lawn. (nu)

Useful expressions

What’s up? · What are you doing? · I’m doing the dishes. · Look, it’s raining! · Please be quiet — the baby is sleeping.
